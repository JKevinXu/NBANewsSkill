# NBA News Source Guide

Use this guide to keep NBA briefings accurate and useful.

## Official / primary sources

Prefer these for facts that must be confirmed:

- NBA official site: https://www.nba.com/news
- Official NBA scoreboard JSON: https://cdn.nba.com/static/json/liveData/scoreboard/todaysScoreboard_00.json
- NBA standings: https://www.nba.com/standings
- NBA schedule: https://www.nba.com/schedule
- NBA transactions: https://www.nba.com/players/transactions
- NBA injury report: https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2025-26-season/
- Team official sites and team PR accounts

## Reliable context sources

Use these to add reporting context, but distinguish reporting from official confirmation:

- ESPN NBA
- The Athletic NBA
- Yahoo Sports NBA
- CBS Sports NBA
- AP / Reuters wire stories
- Local beat reporters with direct team access

## Claim handling

- Trades/signings/suspensions: do not call final until official or reported by multiple highly reliable sources.
- Injuries: prefer official injury report, team announcements, coach quotes, and beat reporter practice notes.
- Rumors: label as rumored/reported and include who reported it.
- Standings/playoff claims: verify against official standings or current box scores.
- Fantasy advice: separate confirmed availability from speculation.

## Minimum citation standard

For every news item include:

- Source name
- Publication/update date when available
- Link
- Whether the item is official, reported, or rumored
